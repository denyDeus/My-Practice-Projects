A multidisciplinary career anchored in science, governance and community resilience.

Bertha Magomere is a dedicated Hydrogeologist & Accountant advancing sustainable groundwater solutions, climate resilience, and gender-inclusive community empowerment.

Trained in both Water Resource Management (Water Institute) and Accounting & Finance (Tumaini University), Bertha bridges the gap between field hydrology and financial governance. She applies specialized geophysical survey tools (IPI2Win, Interpex, PQWT detectors, magnetometers) alongside fiscal accounting to ensure that water infrastructure projects are both scientifically robust and financially viable.

As the Founder and CEO of Mimi Ni Maji Hub, an alumna of the SheTech Incubator, participant in the Girls Takeover initiative, and delegate at the East Africa Future Generation Tribunal, Bertha actively champions youth-led and gender-transformative climate initiatives across Tanzania.

What Drives My Work

Grounded in community service, grassroots water walks (such as the Dodoma Water Walk for clean water consciousness), and the enduring ethos of Mwalimu J.K. Nyerere on self-reliance and public duty, Bertha is devoted to solving real-world challenges for communities most vulnerable to climate shocks.

02 — TECHNICAL EXPERTISE & TOOLKIT

Rigorous scientific tools and financial disciplines.

Hands-on proficiency across hydrogeological modeling software, geophysical survey instrumentation, and financial management.

GEOPHYSICS & MODELING SOFTWARE

Subsurface Modeling

  • IPI2Win Software: 1D Vertical Electrical Sounding (VES) resistivity curve interpretation & aquifer delineation.
  • Interpex (IX1D): Advanced geoelectrical resistivity data inversion and multi-layer subsurface aquifer modeling.
  • Hydrological Data Analysis: Data interpretation for catchment vulnerability, recharge, and water quality.
GEOPHYSICAL FIELD EQUIPMENT

Field Instruments

  • PQWT Water Detector: Automated multi-frequency natural electric field mapping for groundwater exploration.
  • Proton Magnetometer: Subsurface geological structure, fault mapping, and magnetic anomaly surveys.
  • Field Campaigns (I & II): Practical field borehole siting, resistivity array measurements, and water sampling.
FINANCIAL GOVERNANCE & ACCOUNTING

Finance & Administration

  • Accounting & Finance (Tumaini): Financial accounting, expenditure tracking, budgeting, and audit readiness.
  • Resource Project Governance: Cost estimation and budget planning for donor and community water initiatives.
  • Contract & Grant Compliance: Ensuring accountability and transparent stewardship in environmental projects.
